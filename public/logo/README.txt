  c:\Users\Lenovo\Desktop\FalakiFoundation\public\logo\falaki-foundation-logo.png

  - falaki-foundation-logo.png
  - falaki-foundation-logo.svg
  - falaki-foundation-logo.webp

No stretching, cropping, or color distortion will be applied; the original aspect ratio and transparency will be preserved.
